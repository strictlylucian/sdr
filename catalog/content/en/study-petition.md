The Ministry of Defence sectoral pension office’s form is a scanned page with boxes for identification, correspondence and petition details. Fields have fixed widths and writing space is limited by the sheet. The inspected scan contains no selectable text.

## Why a complete overhaul is needed

This is a scan of a form designed for a sheet of paper. It asks for information without providing the tools of a digital form:

- **The scanned text and boxes are not semantic form fields.** The document supplies neither selectable text nor a labelled, keyboard-operable set of controls. A screen reader cannot use the scan as an ordinary sequence of questions and answers.
- **Fixed widths restrict the person’s answers.** Names, document details and address elements must fit the printed boxes. The petition itself has one fixed rectangle, although the length needed to explain an issue varies from person to person.
- **The page cannot guide correction as information is entered.** It does not provide contextual hints, associated error messages or a structured final check. Problems may instead be discovered later in whichever submission process uses the document.
- **Recreating the sheet as another fixed layout would retain these constraints.** A digital alternative needs readable, reflowing questions and expandable writing space, followed by a real handling and acknowledgement process.

SDR addresses the data-entry structure and reading experience. A pilot would need to connect that interface to the institution’s petition workflow and test whether the wording and requested information are clear and necessary.

## What SDR proposes

Identity, address and describing the request have separate steps. Labels sit above controls, widths adapt to the screen and the petition has a resizable text area. Errors are associated with questions and answers can be reviewed.

## Mapping to the original

| Published document | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and identity document | The first step retains names, personal number, email and document details. |
| County, town, number, block, entrance, floor, apartment | The second step retains these fields. Optional Street is a proposed addition: absent from the inspected PDF. |
| County, capacity, pension file | Separate questions in the final step. |
| Petition text box | A text area without the scanned sheet’s visual writing limit. |

Source fields are retained for a traceable comparison. A pilot should review the need for a personal number, document details and required fields with the institution. File uploads, authentication, signing and petition registration are not implemented.
