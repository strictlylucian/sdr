In the PDF published by Constanța County Police, questions sit inside a continuous paragraph, details use blank lines and the personal number has individual character boxes. The inspected page is a scan without selectable text.

## Why a complete overhaul is needed

A downloadable scan makes the document available online, but it still presents a paper application:

- **The scan offers no selectable questions or interactive form controls.** It does not give a screen reader the question-and-control structure of an HTML form. Enlarging the page magnifies the sheet instead of reflowing its content to the screen.
- **The applicant has to parse a paragraph with blanks.** Names, parents, birth details and address are embedded in continuous prose. Separate, plainly labelled questions would make it easier to find the next answer to provide.
- **Applicant and institutional work are mixed together.** Registration, issued-certificate details and verification results appear on the same sheet as the applicant’s questions. A digital journey can separate what the person supplies from what the institution records.
- **The paper route leaves guidance and corrections outside the form.** Blank lines and character boxes cannot offer autofill, relevant keyboards, inline feedback or a structured answer check. If a process requires printing, handwriting and scanning, those steps also create equipment and assistance needs.

Digitisation means collecting structured answers through an accessible service and connecting them to processing, rather than reproducing this sheet on a screen. The SDR example demonstrates the data-entry part of that change. The existing MAI electronic service remains a separate route for obtaining a certificate.

## What SDR proposes

Personal details, address and the reason for applying become three steps. One identifier field offers a numeric keyboard, dates use native controls and answers can be checked before finishing.

## Mapping to the original

| Published document | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Identity, previous name, parents, birth details | Personal details step, with one birth date field. |
| Address, document series, number and personal number | Address and document are grouped; the personal number moves to identity. |
| Purpose | An open question with room for an answer. |
| Declaration, date and signature | Date and demonstration confirmation, neither a signature nor legal consent to processing. |
| Registration, issued certificate and verification result | Institutional fields, excluded from the applicant journey. |

The study covers an individual applying on their own behalf. Representation, identity checks, issuance and signing are not implemented. [MAI already provides an electronic service](https://hub.mai.gov.ro/serviciu/view?id=88); this study only explores redesigning the published document.
