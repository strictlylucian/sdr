<Aside type="caution" title="Experimental concepts · Independent proposal">
These boards are studies for a possible institutional identity. They do not represent an identity adopted by the Romanian Government or the institutions named in the examples.
</Aside>

The two concepts explore a monochrome eagle alongside a two-line wordmark. They share the same composition, presented in two colour directions: blue and burgundy.

## Balance · Blue

The first board presents a white-on-blue version and its blue-on-white counterpart. The main composition uses “Guvernul României” (Government of Romania). The “Ministerul Afacerilor Externe” (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) example explores how the same relationship between symbol and text accommodates a longer institutional name.

## Balance · Burgundy

The second board retains the same construction and explores a burgundy direction: white on burgundy for the main version, and burgundy on white for its counterpart. The institutional names and proportions remain the same to allow comparison between the two directions.

## Proportions explored

Both boards make the eagle as tall as the text block: a **1:1** ratio. Their notation is **H = 2 lines × 1.15 em**, with a gap of **H/4** between the eagle and the text.

These relationships describe the construction of the concepts shown. They are study hypotheses, rather than approved rules for an institutional identity.

## Status in SDR

The boards are retained as visual exploration material. They are not default library logos and do not change SDR colours, themes or components.

The [Header](/en/components/header/) and [Page](/en/components/page/) components support an emblem supplied by the institution. These studies document a possible identity direction without prescribing it for services using SDR.
