This study redesigns the CAS Bucharest journey for an individual insured under a contract who applies for payment of their own medical leave allowance. Use fictional details and documents throughout the examples.

## What already exists

The [CASMB form](https://www.casmb.ro/atl_uploads_pf_concedii_recuperare.php) starts with the insured person's details and contact information in a table. The [CASMB document list](https://www.casmb.ro/atl_uploads_pf.php) separately asks for the standard application, identity document, medical leave certificate, proof of contribution payment and an IBAN. It also describes sending the original application and medical certificate after the online submission. The study is based on the first public page and the published document list.

## Where applicants face friction

- **Applicants have to consult several pages.** The document list appears in the service directory, while the first form page starts with personal details.
- **Some details also appear in the PDF.** [Annex 9 published by CNAS](https://cnas.ro/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ANEXA-9-Cerere-recuperare-concedii-si-indemnizatii-persoane-fizice.pdf) asks for the applicant's name and personal number again and combines several situations in one document.
- **The process also involves original documents.** CASMB's instructions describe an extra step after online submission.

## What SDR proposes

The form groups questions about the applicant, insurance contract and certificate, supporting documents and payment. Each document has a dedicated upload control. The standalone demo validates only the first step, including the format of the fictional personal number and email address. The summary lets applicants check and change their answers before the simulated confirmation. An integrated service could use the details entered once to populate a structured application.

## What a pilot could establish

CASMB would need to confirm the fields and documents for this case, including when it can accept digital documents in place of originals. User testing could establish whether people know what to prepare before starting and can correct a detail from the summary.
