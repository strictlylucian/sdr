This study concerns a **company’s application to cross public land for works in Alexandria**, handled by the municipal street-services operator ASCE. It is a defined approval service with a project and supporting documents, not a general contact form.

## Check for an existing digital service

Checked on **27 October 2026**: ASCE’s [official approval instructions](https://ascealexandria.ro/avize/) publish separate downloadable applications for individuals and companies and explicitly instruct applicants to submit the application and dossier at its office. The service catalogue, linked site navigation and official-service searches did not identify a dedicated online application for this approval. That is a bounded check of published services, not proof about an internal or forthcoming platform; ASCE should confirm the position before any pilot.

ASCE [states that it was established by Alexandria’s Local Council](https://ascealexandria.ro/about-us/). The example concerns a municipal public-service operator, not a central ministry.

## What the published form asks for

The company application is a Word document: company details, representative, urban-planning certificate, work location, designer and project, supporting documents, date and signature. The original Word download is linked below. The source image shows the **service instructions and download links**, rather than pretending to be an image of the Word page.

## Why a complete overhaul is needed

- **The published route still requires a dossier at an office.** A downloadable editable file helps prepare it, but does not provide a structured online application, document completeness checks or status tracking.
- **Questions are embedded in a formal paragraph.** Certificate number, date, issuer, work description, street, project number and designer details are blanks inside sentences. Separate questions make the required information easier to identify and check.
- **The document and service page give different office numbers.** The Word form names Libertății 458; the current service instructions specify 507A. Reusing old document text without checking the live service can send applicants to the wrong place. The prototype links the current instructions and performs no dispatch.
- **Supporting documents need visible, contextual handling.** The written checklist includes project documents, urban-planning and ownership evidence, and delegation documents where applicable. These should be visible requirements with clear upload points, rather than an undifferentiated attachment.

## What SDR proposes

Four panels separate company/representative details, the work and project, documents, and signatory/date. This study follows the **company Word form**; the separate individual form remains linked from the institution’s service page. The document panel keeps the requirements visible and reveals delegation documents when that option is selected. Upload formats and the 5 MB per-file limit are explicit prototype choices, not source upload rules. No application, signature, file or payment is transmitted.

## Mapping to the original

| Source document or published instruction | SDR proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Company name, registered office, county, register number and tax code | First panel, plus the representative’s name. |
| Urban-planning certificate, work location and project/designer | Separate labelled fields and expandable descriptions. |
| Project memorandum and plans, certificate and ownership document | Individual document controls. |
| Identity/registration and delegation evidence | Company registration follows the current service-page checklist; delegation documents appear when applicable. The institution must reconcile the Word form’s older identity wording. |
| Date and signature | Date and signatory name for inspecting the composition; no signature is created or validated. |

## What a pilot could establish

ASCE would first need to confirm the absence of an existing equivalent and reconcile the document, address and service checklist. A pilot could then test dossier completeness, delegation, secure document handling, fees, technical review and status updates. Recreating the questions alone does not digitise the approval decision.
