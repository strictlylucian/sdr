The starting point is **DGPCI’s existing web form for provisional authorisation fees**, at its public payment-type-12 route. The study covers the data-entry stage.

## What already exists

The form requests email and confirmation, applicant type, recipient and payer identifiers, then document quantity. On desktop, long labels sit in a narrow column separate from their controls.

## Why a complete overhaul is needed

This interface needs a complete overhaul. Its layout makes a simple payment task unnecessarily difficult to read and check.

- **Cramped labels obscure the questions.** Recipient and payer labels wrap across a narrow side column, forcing people to match fragments of text to the right field.
- **Essential guidance fails text-contrast requirements.** The privacy and email notice has approximately **3.03:1** contrast, below the **4.5:1** minimum in [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html).
- **Recipient and payer are easily confused.** Similar identity questions need distinct groups and clear explanations of whose details belong where.

The same basic questions look completely different in the IGI service. Citizens pay the cost of this inconsistency every time they move between institutions.

## What SDR proposes

Recipient, contact and quantity become separate steps. Email uses the appropriate native input type, the confirmation field is retained and the demonstration total updates when quantity changes. Labels sit above controls and can wrap without compressing a separate label column.

## Mapping to the original

| Existing web form | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Applicant type | A dropdown with individual and legal-entity options. The selection controls the recipient fields. |
| Recipient and recipient identifier | Full name and fictional CNP for an individual; company name and fictional CUI/CIF for a legal entity. Only the selected branch is shown and included in the review. |
| Payer identifier | A separate field, retained even when payer and recipient may be the same person. Equality is not assumed. |
| Email and confirmation | Email and confirmation grouped in the second step. |
| Document quantity and amount | Quantity and an automatically updated total. |
| reCAPTCHA and continuing to payment | Not simulated as completed checks. The demonstration ends after answer review. |
